## About

I work at the boundary between software and hardware: Bluetooth LE, ADB, HID, Linux kernel configuration, and device-specific quirks. I like turning “there is no app for this” into something people can install and use.

Most of my work is open source, local-first, and built around giving you control over hardware you already own.

> [!TIP]
> New here? Start with **[DualSense UI](https://github.com/Hitomatito/dualsenseUI)** — a GTK4 control center for DualSense and DualSense Edge controllers on Linux.

## Featured projects

### [DualSense UI](https://github.com/Hitomatito/dualsenseUI)

**A proper control center for DualSense on Linux.** Configure the lightbar, adaptive triggers, audio, battery, and firmware from a native GTK4 app. Available as `.deb`, `.rpm`, and Flatpak, built and tested in CI.

`Python` · `GTK4` · `Linux` · `Flatpak` · `CI`

### [AmWF](https://github.com/Hitomatito/AmWF)

**Monitor mode and packet injection on Snapdragon phones — without rebuilding the firmware.** A Kotlin Android app for Qualcomm-based devices, with 2,000+ APK downloads.

`Kotlin` · `Android` · `Qualcomm` · `Root`

### [HardWire](https://github.com/Hitomatito/HardWire)

**A hardware inspector for Android, local or over USB/Wi-Fi ADB.** Explore 10 hardware categories, compare devices, keep snapshots, and look up SoC details in the built-in database.

`Kotlin` · `Jetpack Compose` · `Material 3` · `ADB`
